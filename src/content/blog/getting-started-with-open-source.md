## Why Open Source Matters

Contributing to open source changed how I write software. Working with **OpenMRS**, **Mifos**, **Open Elements**, **Sugar Labs**, and the **Linux Foundation** taught me to read large codebases, write tests, and collaborate across time zones.

## Finding Your First Issue

1. Pick a project you actually use or care about (healthcare, education, fintech).
2. Read the `CONTRIBUTING.md` and join the community chat.
3. Start with documentation fixes or "good first issue" labels.
4. Ask questions early — mentors expect it.

## What I Learned at OpenMRS

- Healthcare software demands rigor: every line can affect patient data workflows.
- Small PRs get reviewed faster than massive refactors.
- Consistency in commit messages and issue linking builds trust with maintainers.

## Advice for Students

You do not need to be an expert. Show up consistently, learn Git workflow, and treat feedback as a gift. Your university projects and open source work together tell a strong story on your resume.
