## Starting the Journey

Entering **Bugema University** as a Software Engineering student felt like stepping into a workshop where theory meets practice. From day one, I knew I wanted to build real systems — not just pass exams.

## Coursework That Shaped Me

- **Programming fundamentals** in Java solidified OOP thinking.
- **Database modules** connected directly to school accounting and records projects.
- **Networking and web** courses pushed me toward REST APIs and modern frontends.

## Building Beyond the Classroom

While classmates focused only on assignments, I paired coursework with:

- Client projects for **Kitoma Secondary School** and **Rakai Community School of Nursing**
- Evening open source sessions with global communities
- Learning TypeScript and React to level up frontend skills

## Community on Campus

Study groups, hackathon ideas, and peer code reviews made Bugema more than lectures. Sharing what I learned from open source inspired others to contribute too.

## Looking Ahead (Year 2)

Year two means deeper algorithms, more team projects, and stronger portfolio pieces. I am aiming to merge healthcare-tech experience from OpenMRS with full-stack skills in Django and Spring Boot.
